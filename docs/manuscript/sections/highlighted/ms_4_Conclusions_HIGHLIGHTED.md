## 4. Conclusions

This study demonstrates that coupling leak-free feature engineering with calibrated non-linear ensembles substantially outperforms conventional mathematical baselines across both validation and temporal holdout regimes. Non-parametric rank tests verify that observed accuracy and bias gains are statistically robust rather than artifacts of sample noise.

Future research should prioritize: (1) multi-source covariate fusion from remote-sensing or physical reanalysis products to narrow uncertainty in sparse subregions; (2) probabilistic quantile or conformal prediction frameworks for extreme-event risk bounds; and (3) lightweight deployable pipelines for near-real-time operational monitoring.
